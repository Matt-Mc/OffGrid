# Design context

Confirmed with the project owner during the UI refresh planning.

- Audience: personal use, especially preparing videos for travel and watching without a connection.
- Primary jobs: save videos before a trip, see which downloads are ready, watch and resume local videos, and keep disk use predictable.
- Tone: quiet, native Mac feel; comfortable, restrained, and easy to understand.
- Identity: Offgrid's muted green accent, warm neutral surfaces, familiar controls, subtle separators, and thumbnail-led browsing.
- Priorities: dependable downloads and storage controls, clear status and recovery, keyboard accessibility, and preservation of saved videos.
- Avoid: dashboard clutter, excessive cards, technical downloader details in everyday flows, and automatic deletion without an explicit policy.
